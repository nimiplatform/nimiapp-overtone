# The living score

Mode: Operate. The user approves musical exploration and explicitly authorizes a complete UI/UX redesign, free of the incumbent style. All product and design decisions are delegated; no further interview is needed. This iteration builds directly in code so the signature interaction, not a static comp, sets the quality bar.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Musical exploration is a playable score. A gesture expresses intent, three interpretations invite comparison, and recordings stay within reach.

OWN-WORLD: Paper-white working surfaces, ink-black lettering, a saturated ultramarine score field and acid-yellow intent marker. Crisp planar surfaces replace ambient glass. Strong Archivo lettering, generous content spacing and precise compact labels. Light serves daytime desktop exploration; dark keeps equivalent role contrast for evening listening.

STORY: Enter directly into the playground, describe a scene, move energy and surprise on the score, explore three real AI proposals, browse and apply one, then generate and audition beside the other recordings. Notes and lyrics open only when wanted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A quiet brand header with one preferences disclosure; a spacious creation stage takes the left two thirds, a permanent recordings shelf the right third. The idea composer sits above a blue interactive score and a three-choice interpretation index. The named generation target sits immediately below. A compact full-width listening instrument anchors the footer. Dragging the score updates both intent values with immediate geometric response; keyboard and accessible range inputs provide the same control. Recordings are real waveform rows with direct A/B assignment.

FORM: The playable graphic score, candidate 7, seed b4eea017. Grounded alternatives considered: music-poster wall, analog sampler, score atlas, folded track sleeves, listening-room projection, radio tuner, playable graphic score. Challengers: iridescent cloud declined (raise disciplined color placement); gravity-rain garden competitive (raise direct-manipulation feedback); seedbed-lobes declined (raise whole-region color commitment); transit diagram competitive (raise explicit relationships); HyperCard declined (raise in-place editability); split-flap board declined (raise stable recording-row anatomy). These are execution standards, not borrowed decorative costumes. The signature uses mathematical graphics and real interactions, not shipping raster assets.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
